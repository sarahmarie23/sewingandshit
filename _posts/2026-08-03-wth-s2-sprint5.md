## 🎀 Corset

![Corset done](/assets/images/wth/s2/20260730_corset_done.jpg)

**Status** - It works. Some tiny things can be improved.

* There's a pocket behind each flap. I didn't realize until *after* the ren faire that the pocket opening should come from the side, not the top. You know, liek a regular pants pocket or jacket pocket.

* The velcro holding on that decoration piece might need to be reglued.

* If I have time, I would like to give the corset a paint job like the pauldrons. Show it some love.

* Because I ran out of time, I hot glued the purple flap. Making it velcro would be much better.

* The purple sash along the bottom is far too short. Double it's length.

## 💄 Makeup
![Tattoo test](/assets/images/wth/s2/20260730_tattoo_test.jpg)

**Status** - I got over my airbrush anxiety (YAY!), learned how to use the Cricut Joy2 (the perfect little purchase for my use case), made a stencil and it worked!

* The dark lipstick isn't what I will wear. I only wore it to the renaissance faire because the other lipstick that I used looked weird with this new shade of purple.

* If I can figure out how to get alcohol liquid makeup to work, that would be AMAZING. This stuff was good, but for the rest of my body, alcohol would last longer.

![Makeup after](/assets/images/wth/s2/20260801_makeup_after.jpg)
*After a day at the renaissance faire, my makeup was better than I expected. I didn't expect the lipstick to last. Dark lipstick never does. That's why I will find another shade that will work with me.*

Let me just add that I was *very* impressed with how my eyebrows and ears held up, despite them smacking everything as I drove. I was not prepared for that annoyance.
