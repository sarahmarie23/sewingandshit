---

## 🔮 Thoughts for Future Work

- Even without the headband and just the wire, the ears seem to stay in place, but could use more experimenting with where to use the Prosaide.
- I expected that I would be upset after breaking the blade wire. I felt more disillusioned than anything.
- Give extra slack in wires.
- Don't solder wires at a point where they will receive stress. I did put heat shrink tubing, but it probably wasn't tight enough. The wires inside broke, but they were very thin.
- Thin wires were good for the Dark Heart, but the blade could/should have used thicker wires.
